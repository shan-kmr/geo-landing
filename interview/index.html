Benchmarking: Lifecycle of an order

A quick commerce order passes through five location steps: a typed address, a geocode, driver position, a trail in transit, an arrival. We measured a frontier language model on each step, against the deterministic systems that do the job today, on the same ground truth.

4The trail in transit

48 trails built from real Android GNSS drives: 16 clean, 16 with an injected 2 to 3 km teleport, 16 where position advances while the device reports itself stationary. Raw pings in, verdict out.

The model reads trails well. It caught every injected fraud. It also accused 3 honest couriers, once at confidence 80. The physics check (per-ping implied speed, plus net displacement against device-reported speed over a 60 s window) is exact on all 48, costs nothing, and its reasons are auditable. At fleet scale the 19% false-fraud rate is the operational number.

The internal map is fame-indexed. The Empire State Building's own address pins within 71 m on every tier. Random residential Queens doors from the same file land 600 to 3,700 m out. Linear probes on open models show the same shape: place coordinates read out of the residual stream down to town level, and the margin over a random-init floor collapses at venue level (Gurnee & Tegmark 2310.02207, replicated and extended on-device in the mappa repo).

Quick commerce lives entirely on the unfamous tail.

The read

Each stage has a deterministic system that answers in milliseconds with meter accuracy. The model answers the same questions within a few hundred meters, in a confident tone, and the failures cluster where money moves: the typed address, the door, the fraud call on an honest courier.

The model is a capable orchestrator over these steps. It read raw trails competently, respected the wrong-door rule, and ranked known places almost perfectly. The working division of labor gives the model the language and the judgment, and gives the ground to deterministic tools it can call.

That is the API: geocoding with an honest interval, presence and dwell verdicts, trail integrity checks, served to agents as tools.

Method. Subject model claude-sonnet-5, effort medium, JSON-schema outputs, batched prompts, no tool use, parametric knowledge only. 21 subagents, 353 items, one run, 2026-08-27. Seeds and injection parameters recorded. Truth: NYC AddressPoint (967,871 doors, incl. 340,730 hyphenated Queens), OSM/Nominatim for extras, GNSS traces from public Android datasets (MTV/SF). Engine columns: recorded runs of pinned local Nominatim 5.3.2 / Photon 1.2.1 / Pelias (NYC build), plus the public Nominatim API for messy and reverse queries (1 req/s, cached). Pelias ingests the city address file, which is why it is near exact; that is the point of an engine.

Caveats. One model family at one date; published results on GPT-4 class models show the same shape (GPT4GEO 2306.00020, geographic bias 2402.02680, spatial distortions 2401.04218). Fraud and dwell labels are constructed by injection into real traces; classes are separable by design and harder adversaries exist. Venue-tier items are NYC playgrounds and Indian venues; n per slice is small. Ghost "found" for engines counts any returned candidate, mostly honest street fallbacks.
